# Technology capabilities

Technology capabilities describe what technology must do to enable Defra's business capabilities. This page shows the current Level 1 and Level 2 capability structure.

!!! info "How to use this map"

    Use the Level 1 areas to understand the broad technology landscape. The cards beneath each area show its Level 2 capabilities. Supporting guardrails, standards and reference architectures can be linked as that content is published.
